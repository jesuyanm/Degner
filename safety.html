On site

Safety Protocols

  • Daily Safety Briefings

    Every workday begins with a tailgate safety meeting covering hazards, weather, and emergency procedures.

  • Training & Certifications

    Crews hold the tickets their work calls for, including ground disturbance, confined space, H2S Alive, fall protection, and first aid.

  • Trench & Excavation Safety

    Shoring, trench boxes, and sloping are planned and checked so every excavation is safe before anyone enters.

  • Ground Disturbance & Locates

    Utility locates and hand-exposure procedures are completed before any digging begins.

  • Equipment Inspections

    Operators complete a pre-use inspection on every machine, and anything defective is tagged out until repaired.

  • Emergency Response Plans

    Every site has an emergency plan with muster points, first aid, and contacts, reviewed with each crew.

  • Incident Reporting & Investigation

    All near-misses and incidents are immediately investigated and analyzed to prevent recurrence.

  • Contractor Safety Requirements

    All subcontractors must meet our safety standards — training, insurance, and SMS compliance — before setting foot on site.

  • Environmental Protection

    Erosion and sediment control, spill kits, and careful handling of fuels protect surrounding land and waterways.
